# Evidence Card

## Source

Citation: Kim, J., Park, J., Lee, H., Ko, M., & Lee, U. (2019). LocknType: Lockout Task Intervention for Discouraging Smartphone App Use. CHI 2019.

Link: https://pure.kaist.ac.kr/en/publications/lockntype-lockout-task-intervention-for-discouraging-smartphone-a/

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300927

## Source Type

- primary study
- in-situ HCI experiment
- digital self-control / interaction restraint

## Research Context

The study tested whether adding a lockout task before opening target smartphone apps would change launch decisions. Workloads included pause-only, 10-digit input, and 30-digit input.

## Main Finding

Access friction discouraged app use in a dose-shaped way. A pause-only task discouraged a measurable share of app launches, and heavier input discouraged much more.

## Empirical Detail

- Sample: 40 participants.
- Duration: three-week in-situ experiment.
- Conditions: pause-only, 10-digit input, 30-digit input.
- Pause-only discouraged an average of 13.1% of app use.
- The 30-digit input task discouraged 47.5% of app use.

## Non-Obvious Mechanism

The intervention worked before the user entered the tempting app. This suggests that a small access cost can interrupt automatic launch behavior without needing a full block.

## Limitations

This studied smartphones and app launches, not browser chains. It measured discouraged app use, not whether the user's replacement activity was better.

## Evidence Grade

moderate for DaD.

## Relevance To DaD

DaD should treat friction as an adjustable dose. Continue-with-reason, cooldowns, delayed Continue, and hard quarantine should not be lumped together as "blocking."

## Design Consequence

Add explicit intervention severity levels:

- prompt only;
- short pause;
- reason required;
- cooldown;
- return-only during strict policy;
- hard quarantine.

## What Changes

DaD can justify intermediate friction before full lockout. This supports stricter prompt states during locked schedules without making every enforcement screen a dead end.
