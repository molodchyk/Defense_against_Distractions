# Evidence Card

## Source

Citation: Dillard, J. P., & Shen, L. (2005). On the Nature of Reactance and its Role in Persuasive Health Communication. Communication Monographs, 72(2), 144-168.

Link: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/03637750500111815

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/03637750500111815

## Source Type

- primary experiments
- persuasive health communication
- reactance measurement

## Research Context

The paper tested alternative models of psychological reactance in health communication, including cognitive, affective, parallel, and intertwined cognitive-affective models.

## Main Finding

The intertwined model best fit the data: reactance was represented by both anger and negative cognitions.

## Empirical Detail

- Contexts included alcohol consumption and flossing messages.
- Freedom threat was experimentally manipulated as high versus low.
- Competing models tested whether reactance was cognitive, affective, parallel, or intertwined.
- The intertwined model fit best across both health topics.
- Later reviews describe anger and negative cognitions as contributing about equally to the motivation to restore freedom.

## Non-Obvious Mechanism

Reactance is not only irritation. It is also counterarguing, which helps explain why a user may not merely dislike DaD enforcement but start building reasons to defeat or reject it.

## Limitations

The study is about persuasive health messages, not self-imposed browser locks. It supports mechanism, not DaD thresholds.

## Evidence Grade

strong for reactance measurement; moderate for DaD.

## Relevance To DaD

DaD should not measure enforcement health only through immediate action completion. It should also watch for patterns consistent with counterarguing or source rejection.

## Design Consequence

Local diagnostics should distinguish:

- compliant Return after prompt;
- Continue followed by deeper drift;
- repeated graph inspection without recovery;
- repeated emergency escape;
- settings weakening during lock;
- disable/re-enable signals where available.

## What Changes

Bypass pressure becomes a research-backed product metric, not a character judgment.

## Notes

This card pairs with Rains 2013, which meta-analytically supports the intertwined reactance model.
