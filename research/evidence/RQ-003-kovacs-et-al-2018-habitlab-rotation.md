# Evidence Card

## Source

Citation: Kovacs, G., Wu, Z., & Bernstein, M. S. (2018). Rotating Online Behavior Change Interventions Increases Effectiveness But Also Increases Attrition. Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction, CSCW.

Link: https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/2018/habitlab/habitlab-cscw18.pdf

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3274364

## Source Type

- primary field experiments
- browser extension study
- online behavior change systems

## Research Context

HabitLab tested whether rotating between behavior-change interventions works better than static interventions for reducing time online.

## Main Finding

Rotating interventions increased effectiveness as measured by time on site, but also increased attrition. A just-in-time information design about rotation reduced attrition rates by about half.

## Empirical Detail

- Platform: HabitLab browser extension.
- Design: three in-the-wild field experiments.
- Outcome: time on site and uninstall attrition.
- Rotation improved behavioral effectiveness but led more users to uninstall.
- A just-in-time explanation about intervention rotation reduced attrition rates by half.

## Non-Obvious Mechanism

The more effective intervention can be the one users abandon if it violates their mental model. Explanation is not only politeness; it can protect retention when intervention behavior changes.

## Limitations

The study concerns rotating interventions, not locked schedules. It does not prove which DaD explanations are sufficient.

## Evidence Grade

strong for browser-extension intervention design; moderate for DaD lock escalation.

## Relevance To DaD

DaD has multiple intervention modes. If escalation feels unpredictable, users may experience the extension as hostile even when it is helping.

## Design Consequence

Every escalation should expose:

- what changed;
- why it changed;
- which policy caused it;
- what the user can do now.

## What Changes

DaD should treat explanation as part of the intervention itself, especially when moving from prompt to quarantine or from editable settings to locked settings.

## Notes

This source argues against hidden cleverness. A stronger system needs a clearer mental model.
