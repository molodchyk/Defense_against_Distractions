# Evidence Card

## Source

Citation: Hightower, R. R., Ring, L. T., Helfman, J. I., Bederson, B. B., & Hollan, J. D. (1998). Graphical Multiscale Web Histories: A Study of PadPrints. Proceedings of Hypertext 1998, 58-65.

Link: https://www.cs.umd.edu/projects/hcil/pad%2B%2B/papers/hypertext-98-padprints/hypertext-98-padprints.pdf

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/276627.276634

## Source Type

- primary study
- HCI
- web history visualization

## Research Context

The authors built PadPrints, a browser companion that dynamically generated a graphical map of visited pages, then tested it in usability studies.

## Main Finding

Graphical web history improved revisitation tasks. In tasks requiring returns to prior pages, PadPrints users completed tasks in 61.2 percent of the time required by users of the same browser without PadPrints. Users also accessed fewer pages and reported higher satisfaction.

## Empirical Detail

- Tool: graphical multiscale history map.
- Studies: two usability studies.
- Key effect: return-to-prior-page tasks took 61.2 percent of baseline time with PadPrints.
- Additional outcome: significant decreases in pages accessed.
- Satisfaction: overall satisfaction in one reported experiment rose from 5.883 without PadPrints to 8.087 with PadPrints on the reported scale.
- Context: controlled web navigation/revisitation tasks.

## Non-Obvious Mechanism

The graph is not merely "explainability." A usable history representation can directly reduce navigation work and help users return.

## Limitations

The study is old, used a different browser era, and focused on general web navigation rather than digital self-control.

## Evidence Grade

strong for graphical history supporting revisitation; moderate to strong for DaD Show graph.

## Relevance To DaD

DaD's Show graph action can be a recovery instrument if it shows the current trajectory clearly and supports action.

## Design Consequence

The intent graph should be compact and action-paired. Show graph should expose Return/Isolate actions, not just static diagnostics.

## What Changes

This upgrades Show graph from transparency-only to intervention support.
