Defense Against Distractions helps you protect your attention when ordinary website blocking is not enough.

It is very easy to distract yourself on the internet and procrastinate. There are thousands of video games to play, news stories to read, videos to watch, chats to continue, and feeds to refresh. Often this is exactly what we do when a project deadline or exam still feels far away.

Many focus tools block websites by URL. That helps, but it cannot cover everything. A useful website can still become distracting depending on what you are doing there. For example, even on ChatGPT you could talk about news, video games, drama, or anything else when you do not feel like studying or working.

With this extension, you can block pages based on words and phrases that appear in their text. You create groups of websites and keywords. When a page reaches the configured distraction score, the extension blocks it.

Current features:

Block pages based on keywords, phrases, and weighted keyword scores.
Organize websites and keywords into separate groups.
Apply multiple groups to the same website.
Use locked schedules to make focus rules harder to weaken during chosen time windows.
Whitelist websites that should never be scanned.
Protect the options page with a password.
Hide distracting UI elements with a popup-based element picker.
Export and import extension settings.
Use light, dark, or system UI mode.

Defense Against Distractions is open source:
https://github.com/molodchyk/Defense_against_Distractions.
